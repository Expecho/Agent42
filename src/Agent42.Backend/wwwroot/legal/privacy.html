Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Agent 42 (chat.agent42.nl) is an AI chat service. This page explains what the service stores, where it goes and what you can do about it.

Agent 42 is invite-only. You can only sign in with a Microsoft account that the operator has explicitly allowed.

What we store

  • Your account identity. When you sign in, Microsoft Entra ID passes us your account id, name, email address and roles. We use them to keep your data separate from other users' data.
  • What you create. Your conversations and messages, uploaded images and documents, stored agents, skills, groups, pins, share links and preferences.
  • Your settings for other services. The model providers and MCP servers you add, with their API keys and tokens. Keys are stored encrypted.
  • Push subscriptions. If you turn on notifications, we store the address your browser's push service gives us.
  • Technical logs. Requests, errors and performance data, labelled with your account identity, which the operator uses to troubleshoot.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in the operator's Microsoft Azure subscription. Microsoft processes this data on our behalf.

Who else receives your data

Agent 42 has no built-in AI model. To use it, you add your own model providers, and the services below only receive data when you use the matching feature.

  • Your model providers (for example Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Anthropic or GitHub Models). They receive the content of the conversation you are having with them, including attachments, tool results and documents relevant to your question. Because you use your own account, the provider's own terms and privacy policy apply.
  • MCP servers you connect. They receive whatever the model sends to their tools.
  • Web search. Search queries go through a search proxy that we host ourselves and are forwarded to Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. Those engines see the query, but not your identity or your IP address.
  • Voice input. Speech recognition runs in your browser. Some browsers, such as Chrome, send the audio to their own vendor to transcribe it. Read-aloud uses the speech model of your own provider.
  • Location. Only when you have allowed it and the model asks for it, your browser shares your coordinates. They are passed to your model provider and stored in that conversation like any other tool result.
  • Push notifications. Notifications are delivered through your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft). The content is encrypted end to end, so the push service cannot read it.
  • Webhooks and inbound email. If you give a stored agent a webhook, every conversation turn of that agent is sent to the URL you set. If you use an agent's email address, incoming mail is received by Mailgun and passed to us.
  • Share links. Anyone who has a share link can read that conversation until you revoke the link.
  • Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the app loads.

We do not sell your data, show advertising or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Cookies and local storage

We set one session cookie to keep you signed in. Your browser also stores your preferences and an offline copy of the app on your own device.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations and other content stay until you delete them.
  • Incognito chats are never stored.
  • In the continuous chat of a stored agent, messages older than 24 hours are deleted automatically.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic traces are kept only as long as troubleshooting needs them.
  • To have your whole account deleted, contact us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export and delete your data, and object to or restrict how we process it. You can export and delete your conversations yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
